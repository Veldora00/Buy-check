Buy Check – Privacy

Last updated 19 November 2026

Buy Check is a browser extension that compares Australian home, investment and commercial property loans for the property you're viewing.

What stays in your browser

These are kept only in your browser's storage on your device. They are never sent to us or anyone else:

  • the property's address, price and details
  • your deposit, interest rate, loan term and other numbers you type
  • the list of homes you looked at
  • the pages and websites you visit

What we count

To see how Buy Check is used, we count, anonymously:

  • when the Buy Check panel is opened;
  • when a lender link is clicked – only the lender's name, whether you chose live-in, investment or commercial, and whether you were on a home or commercial property site.

Each is added to a daily total. We don't record your IP address, name, email, any identifier for you or your device, the property, or any page address. The totals are stored in a database hosted by Supabase.

You can turn this off: at the bottom of the Buy Check panel, click Privacy and untick Share anonymous counts.

Data we read

Loan products come from banks' public product information published under the Consumer Data Right.

Property details come from the page you're viewing on domain.com.au, realestate.com.au, realcommercial.com.au or commercialrealestate.com.au, and are read inside your browser only.

Lender links

Links to lenders' websites include utm_source=buycheck so lenders can see the visit came from Buy Check. Nothing about you or the property is added. Once you're on a lender's website, its own privacy policy applies.

Sharing and selling

We don't sell or share any data, and we don't use it for advertising or to decide anyone's creditworthiness.

Not advice

Buy Check gives general information and estimates only – not credit or financial advice. Check rates, fees and eligibility with the lender or a licensed credit adviser.
