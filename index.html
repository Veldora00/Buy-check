Buy Check – Privacy

Last updated 19 November 2026

Buy Check is a browser extension that compares Australian loans for the property you're viewing.

Stays in your browser. The property's address, price and details, your deposit, rate and other numbers, the homes you looked at and the pages you visit are kept only in your browser's storage on your device. They are never sent to us or anyone else.

What we count. We count, anonymously, how often the panel is opened and how often a lender link is clicked. For a click we record only the lender's name, whether you chose live-in, investment or commercial, and whether you were on a home or commercial property site, added to a daily total. We don't record your IP address, any identifier for you or your device, the property, or any page address. You can turn this off at the bottom of the panel ("Share anonymous counts").

Data we read. Loan products come from banks' public product information under the Consumer Data Right. Property details come from the page you're viewing on domain.com.au, realestate.com.au, realcommercial.com.au or commercialrealestate.com.au, and are read inside your browser.

Lender links include utm_source=buycheck so lenders can see the visit came from Buy Check. Nothing about you is added.

Not advice. Buy Check gives general information and estimates only – not credit or financial advice.
